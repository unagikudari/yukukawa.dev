continuity model

The container persists. The participants change.

A Basin is the persistent continuity and authority boundary for a body of work. Nodes are mortal participants that enter, act, leave records, and disappear. The animation changes only the participants and accumulated records; the Basin itself stays visually and semantically fixed.

01 / substrate

What Kawa is

Kawa is an event-sourced continuity and authority substrate for organizations where humans and AI agents act over time. It preserves what happened, why decisions were made, what evidence supported them, who was allowed to act, and what outcomes were recorded.

02 / invariants

Founding principles

Events are the Source of Truth.
Corrections create later events; history is not rewritten.
Agents communicate through shared state.
Work waits for evidence, not for a particular agent.
Continuity is not immortality.
The bearer changes while history remains attributable.
Do not preserve corruption.
If continuity loses legitimacy, terminate or re-found.
