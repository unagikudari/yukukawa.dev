01 / succession

Kawa is the river of succession.

Kawa is an event-sourced continuity and authority substrate for organizations where humans and AI agents act over time. But continuity is not immortality. Humans leave. Agents terminate. Missions finish, fail, or lose legitimacy. Authority expires. Views and narratives dissolve and are rebuilt.

Kawa preserves the history from which a successor can judge whether to continue, terminate, or re-found. A dead participant does not automatically justify a living mission, and durable history does not make old authority operative forever.

PARTICIPANTSAgents, humans, runtimes, and nodes may disappear or be replaced.
MISSIONSMay be fulfilled, withdrawn, invalidated, abandoned, or re-founded.
AUTHORITYExpires, revokes, and must not survive merely because its record does.
UNDERSTANDINGViews, SA, summaries, and narratives are reconstructed by each observer.
HISTORYEvents remain as attributable evidence of what was recorded and learned.

Not everything should continue. What should continue must be earned.

  • Replace a participant without losing the work.Claude, Codex, Gemini, local models, humans, nodes, and runtimes are participants — not the memory.
  • Do not inherit dead authority.An approval or capability can remain in history while no longer authorizing anything now.
  • Allow missions to die.Completion, invalidation, withdrawal, and abandonment are valid outcomes — not failures of continuity.
  • Death is part of learning.Failed plans, ended missions, and replaced participants remain evidence for successors without becoming commands to them.
  • Do not treat AI inference as fact.Observation and Claim are distinct, attributed records. Unknown is a valid standing.
  • Keep working through partitions.Divergent histories are preserved and reconciled, never silently overwritten.
  • Evidence without exposure.Records bind to sources by digest, never by copying — credentials authenticate collection but never enter the durable record.
  • Succession is not cloning.A fresh observer rebuilds Situational Awareness from current evidence instead of inheriting a predecessor's narrative as truth.
03 / invariants

Founding principles

Events are the Source of Truth.
Corrections create later events; history is not rewritten.
Historical persistence ≠ semantic immortality.
A record may remain while its authority, freshness, or operative force has ended.
Continuity must be justified.
A successor validates mission, plan, authority, and evidence before continuing.
Derived views organize records; they do not become reality.
Projections, SA, narratives, indexes, and caches are rebuildable.
Stable semantics. Replaceable mechanics.
Storage, transport, runtimes, and CLI vendors must remain swappable.
Succession is not cloning.
New observers inherit evidence, not compulsory interpretation.
Do not preserve corruption.
When continuity loses legitimacy, terminate or re-found instead of extending the broken lineage blindly.
Prevent when possible; preserve and reconcile when not.
No manufactured exactly-once illusions.
04 / system

Status
