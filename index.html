succession gate

What can continue should not automatically continue.

When a participant dies, succession is a judgment, not a copy operation. Kawa asks whether continuity is still legitimate.

mission still valid?authority still live?plan still current?evidence still adequate?corruption unresolved?
continuereconcileterminatere-found

Succession is not cloning. Death is also evidence and learning.

Mortality

Agents, humans, missions, authorities, and views all have endings. Historical persistence does not imply semantic immortality.

Basins

Basins can split, coexist, and merge without making Kawa a wider central hub. The public diagram now treats them as braided flows inside a constant-width river envelope.

Why you can drag forever

The river is rendered as three identical periods and re-centered modulo one period while you drag. There is no left or right edge to reach.

01 / substrate

What Kawa is

Kawa is an event-sourced continuity and authority substrate for organizations where humans and AI agents act over time. It preserves what happened, why decisions were made, what evidence supported them, who was allowed to act, and what outcomes were recorded.

Kawa does not promise that everything should continue. It preserves enough durable history for continuity, termination, reconciliation, and re-foundation to be judged.

participant works

↓ succession gate checks mission / authority / plan / evidence
↓ continue | reconcile | terminate | re-found
↓ next participant rebuilds understanding from history
02 / invariants

Founding principles

Events are the Source of Truth.
Corrections create later events; history is not rewritten.
Derived views are disposable.
Understanding is rebuilt; projections do not become reality.
Continuity is not immortality.
Participants, missions, authority, and views may die.
Do not preserve corruption.
If continuity loses legitimacy, terminate or re-found.
Basins preserve divergence.
Branch and reconcile instead of silently overwriting.
Stable semantics, replaceable mechanics.
Models, runtimes, storage, transport, and participants remain swappable.
03 / system

Status

Kawa is pre-alpha and dogfooded during its own development.

The repository is not yet a production security or authority boundary. Status and design claims should remain evidence-backed and component-specific.

Understanding is rebuilt. History remains.
